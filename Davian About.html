Hello, my name is Davian Islas but I'm sure you knew that much. I come from a small town and I am a first generation college student. I have grown up always loving video games, my first console being a Game Boy Advance. It opened my eyes to the creativity people can have with hard work and dedication. I got into storytelling later on in highschool when I was analying true meaning of songs in a class assignment. I love interacting and talking to others, hearing their story really shows you how big this world is. Most people dont share their background, where they came from. And I ask why not? Why not share to others your struggles, your triumphs, and your story. I want to turn those lessons into a creative video game that helps others through there struggles and connnect everyone. This is what I hope to achieve with my games. I hope to touch peoples hearts with my story telling, engage their brain with my gameplay, and ultimatly make them feel something. If I can at least make one person happy with my games or story that would be a win in my book.
