What is Creative Technologies?

The major in Creative Technologies is designed to prepare students for careers in the creative industries. Students will gain a strong foundation in both the technical and artistic aspects of creative technologies, including digital media, interactive design, and multimedia production. The program emphasizes hands-on learning, collaboration, and innovation, equipping graduates with the skills needed to succeed in a rapidly evolving field.
